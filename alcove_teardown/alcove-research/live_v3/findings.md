- LIVE WAVEFORM OBSERVED (clips/live_waveform_blackhole.mp4, strips/live_waveform_blackhole_4fps.png): a single raised bar travels left -> right across the compact glyph as the sweep frequency rises, i.e. bars are frequency bands, low on the left; bars not excited sit as dots at the floor. Log shows MPCProcessAudioTap "ASBD for process tap" / "Starting processing" at 16:47:20 and "Stopping" at 16:47:32 (= the 12 s track).
- Bar geometry (OBSERVED, bars_sweep.csv / bars2.py): the compact live glyph has 5 bars, each 2 px wide at 1x scale (clip x 882-883, 886-887, 889-890, 893-894, 896-897), ~3.5 px pitch, height 2 px (floor, drawn as a dot) up to 12 px max.
- Sweep peak times (clip t): bar1 1.10 s, bar2 3.23 s, bar3 5.28 s, bar4 7.40 s, so ~2.1 s apart = x2.97 in frequency per bar. Clip-to-track offset solved from bar2 (~4.1 s) puts the peaks at ~296 Hz, ~890 Hz, ~2.6 kHz and ~7.7 kHz, matching the geometric centres of the 5-band edge set 20/170/527/1500/4500/10000 Hz (299/890/2598/6708). So the compact glyph uses the 5-band variant (no 81 Hz edge). OBSERVED+STATIC agree.
- Frame cadence: bar heights change every ~2 frames (median, 60 fps capture); the 20 Hz analyzer updates plus the 0.08 s CA tween produce in-between frames.
- Band steps test (steps_test.m4a: 2 s tones at 45/120/300/900/2600/7000 Hz, then a 4 s linear amplitude ramp at 300 Hz; clips/live_waveform_steps.mp4; bars_steps.csv). Clip offset ~ -0.6 s vs track. Mapping OBSERVED: 120 Hz -> bar0 (20-170), 300 Hz -> bar1 (170-527), 900 Hz -> bar2 (527-1500), 2.6 kHz -> bar3 (1500-4500), 7 kHz -> bar4 (4500-10000). Exactly one bar lit per tone, no bleed into neighbours apart from the crossfade frames. 45 Hz segment fell inside the activity's appear animation, so it was not measured.
- Level: a steady sine at 0.8 FS drives the band to the 12 px max. Attack floor->max takes 6-8 frames (100-133 ms), e.g. 2,6,6,10,10,10,12. Release max->floor takes 6-9 frames (100-150 ms), e.g. 12,11,10,10,10,6,6,2. Heights move in ~2-frame (33 ms) steps of about 4 px, so attack and release are roughly symmetric (INFERRED: 20 Hz analyzer frames plus the 0.08 s ease-out tween, with no slow peak-decay).
- Loudness mapping: during the linear 0->0.8 ramp the bar reaches the max within ~0.1-0.2 s of ramp start (amplitude ~0.02-0.04 FS, about -34 to -28 dBFS) and stays saturated. INFERRED: strong dB/log compression with a low floor, so normal music will pin bars near max unless the analyzer's dB range is wide. Apple's analyzer floor/ceiling is still not statically recoverable; this is the empirical bound. (The level reaching the tap also depends on Music's volume slider.)
